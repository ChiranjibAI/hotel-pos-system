Hotel POS System is a premium, offline-first point-of-sale app built for restaurants, cafés, and small food businesses. Designed to run entirely on your device — no internet required, no monthly fees, no cloud account.

◆ WORKS FULLY OFFLINE
All your data — menu, orders, inventory, tables, reports — stays on your device. No internet needed, no server to maintain, no subscription. Your business data never leaves your phone.

◆ TABLE MANAGEMENT
Visual floor plan with color-coded table status (available, occupied, reserved, cleaning). Tap to update status, add tables, and track seat counts. Know your floor at a glance.

◆ KITCHEN ORDER TICKETS (KOT)
Send order tickets to the kitchen with items and quantities only — no prices. The kitchen sees what to cook, the customer sees the bill. Standard restaurant workflow, built in.

◆ SPLIT PAYMENT
Split the bill across Cash, Card, and UPI. Add a tip, track remaining balance, and confirm when fully covered. Perfect for group dining and multi-method payments.

◆ INVENTORY TRACKING
Track ingredients and stock levels. Each order automatically deducts from inventory. Get low-stock alerts so you never run out during service.

◆ CASH REGISTER
Daily cash reconciliation with change calculation. Supports multiple currencies and denominations.

◆ SALES ANALYTICS
Real-time KPI dashboard — today's revenue, order count, average order value. Custom charts for trends, top items, and performance over time.

◆ BLUETOOTH RECEIPT PRINTER
Print receipts via Bluetooth thermal printers. Supports XPrinter (XP-58/76/80), Yokoscan, and CatPrinter/MHT protocols. ESC/POS compatible.

◆ DATA BACKUP
Automatic local backups every 12 hours. Export to CSV, Excel, or Google Sheets. Your data is always safe.

◆ PREMIUM DESIGN
Dark charcoal + gold theme designed for restaurant environments. Plus Jakarta Sans typography. Three theme variants (dark, AMOLED black, light). Built to look professional in front of customers.

◆ PRIVACY FIRST
No personal data collected. No analytics tracking. No ads. The app is completely offline — what happens in your restaurant stays in your restaurant.

Built for restaurants that want a professional POS without the monthly fees.
